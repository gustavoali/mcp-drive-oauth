# Privacy policy - MCP Drive

Last updated: 2026-11-01

MCP Drive is a personal tool that its owner, Gustavo Ali, runs on his own computer to work with his own
Google account. It is not offered to the public.

## Data the app accesses
With the account holder's consent, the app can access Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Calendar
data of the account that authorizes it.

## How the data is used
The data is used only to carry out the requests the account holder makes through the tool. The app does
not sell the data, does not use it for advertising and does not share it with the developer or with
third parties, other than the AI services the account holder chooses to use with the tool.

## Storage
The access tokens are stored on the account holder's computer. The app has no server of its own and
keeps no copy of the data outside that computer.

## Revoking access
Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
